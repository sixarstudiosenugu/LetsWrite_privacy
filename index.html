LetsWrite Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 · Last updated: November 13, 2026

LetsWrite (“the app”) is an offline-first screenwriting app for phone, tablet, computer and the web, made by Onaji Stephen Onche, SIXAR Studios (“SIXAR Studios”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

In short
  • We run no server, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • Your scripts are stored on your device. We cannot see them.
  • Data leaves your device only when you turn on an optional feature (GitHub sync, cloud AI, model or voice downloads) and only goes to the service you chose.
  • You can export or erase everything from inside the app at any time.

1. Information we collect

SIXAR Studios does not collect your personal information. The app has no backend, so nothing you write, set or store is sent to us. There is no sign-up, no login, no advertising identifier, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no third-party tracking code. Fonts are bundled with the app, so opening it does not contact font providers.

The only exception would be if you contact us directly (for example by email or a GitHub issue); we then see whatever you choose to send.

2. What stays on your device

The app stores the following in your browser or app storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on your own device:

  • Your scripts and projects, including title page details, notes, comments, revisions, Beat Board cards, outlines and images you add to the board (images are kept inside the project).
  • Your settings, themes, writing goals, streaks and sprint history, and an optional reminder time.
  • A small count of which commands you use most, used only to arrange the toolbar. It never leaves your device.
  • Optional local diagnostics, if you switch them on. They stay on your device, are scrubbed of tokens, keys, emails and URL query strings, never contain script text, and are shared only if you choose to share them.
  • If you use cloud features, your GitHub token or AI key, stored encrypted with a passphrase that only you know. The passphrase cannot be recovered by us.
  • An optional offline license token, if you paste one in. It is checked on your device, without contacting anyone.

You can optionally encrypt all stored scripts with a passphrase (Menu → Privacy, security and license).

3. Optional features that use the internet

The app works fully offline. The features below connect to other services only if you turn them on or use them. Those services receive your data under their own terms and privacy policies, not ours.

FeatureWhat is sent, and to whom
GitHub sync and sharing Your script files (script.jsonl, project.json, story.json, comments, revisions, a Fountain copy and similar) and your repository details are sent to the repository you choose on GitHub (api.github.com), using a token you create. GitHub and anyone you give access to can see that repository. Your token is never written into a script, repository or export.
Cloud AI (Story Studio) The text you ask the AI to work on (your idea, story, scenes, character notes and surrounding script context) is sent straight from your device to the AI provider you pick (Anthropic, or an OpenAI-compatible service whose address you enter), using your own API key. We never receive it. The provider may process and retain it under its own terms. Off by default.
On-device AI When you choose a model, the model files are downloaded once from public model hosts (such as Hugging Face and GitHub), after you consent. Those hosts see an ordinary download request (for example your IP address). After that, the AI runs on your device and your script is not sent anywhere. Off by default.
Human-like reading voice If you download this optional voice, its model is fetched once from a public model host (Hugging Face) and a small runtime file from the cdn.jsdelivr.net content delivery network. Reading aloud then runs on your device. Off by default.
Dictation and voice commands These use your browser’s or device’s built-in speech recognition. Depending on the browser (for example Chrome), your audio may be processed by that browser vendor’s speech service. The app does not record or store audio and never sends it to us. If you prefer, use your keyboard’s own microphone key or do not use dictation.
Update check (installed apps) The installed Android, iOS, Windows, macOS and Linux apps may download a small public version file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) to learn whether a newer version is required. No script data or identifier is sent; GitHub sees an ordinary request (such as your IP address). The website version does not do this.
Links you open Links such as our website (sixars.com) or an installer download open in your browser and are governed by those sites.
Web hosting If you use the web version, the host (for example GitHub Pages) sees ordinary page requests such as IP address and browser type, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or analyse those logs.

AI output can be inaccurate or unsuitable. AI-written lines are tinted in your script so you can tell them apart, and you can remove the labels at any time. Review everything before you rely on it. Please use an AI key with a spending limit.

4. Device permissions

  • Microphone: only if you use dictation or voice commands, and only when your browser or device asks you.
  • Notifications: only if you set a daily writing reminder.
  • Files and photos: only the files you pick yourself, for example importing a .fdx, Fountain or text-based PDF script, adding an image to the Beat Board, or exporting and sharing a script. Selected files are processed on your device.
  • The app does not request your location, contacts, camera or advertising identifier.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we do not use it to build profiles, because we do not have it. Data is shared with third parties only when you use the optional features in section 3, directly with the service you selected.

6. Security

The app is designed to keep data local and to limit what leaves your device: a strict Content Security Policy, passphrase-based encryption (PBKDF2 with AES-256-GCM) for stored keys and tokens, optional encryption of stored scripts, automatic locking of keys after a period of inactivity, and no secrets in exports, repositories or diagnostics. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure. Keep your passphrase safe (it cannot be recovered), use fine-grained, single-repository GitHub tokens, and keep your device up to date. To report a vulnerability, use GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting on the project repository.

7. Your choices and deleting data

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control of it:

  • Export everything (Menu → Privacy, security and license): all scripts as JSON, never including secrets.
  • Delete everything on this device (same place): removes scripts, keys, settings, offline files and downloaded AI models. Uninstalling the app or clearing site data also removes local data.
  • Data you synced to GitHub is deleted in GitHub (delete the repository or files). Text sent to an AI provider is handled under that provider’s retention and deletion process.
  • Cloud AI, on-device AI, the human-like voice, GitHub sync, diagnostics and reminders are all off until you enable them, and you can switch them off again.

Since SIXAR Studios holds no copy of your data, we have nothing to retrieve or erase on our side.

8. Children

LetsWrite is a general-purpose writing tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age of digital consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under Nigeria’s Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your personal data and to object to its processing. Because we do not collect or hold your personal data, those rights are exercised directly in the app (export and delete) and with the third parties you chose to use. If you contact us and we do hold something about you, we will respond within a reasonable time and in line with applicable law. To raise a complaint with us, email sixarstudiosenugu@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, for example a new optional feature that sends data somewhere, we will update this page and its “Last updated” date before the change ships. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.
